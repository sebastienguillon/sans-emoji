Keep your punctuation.
Skip the suggestions.

A colon should let you finish your sentence. Sans Emoji hides emoji suggestions on Atlassian sites, so you can keep writing.

For HTTPS pages on atlassian.net, atlassian.com and bitbucket.org, including subdomains. Custom company domains are not supported.

Install locally Desktop Chrome and Firefox

Manual installation: build from source with Node.js 24+ and pnpm 12+, then load the extension in your browser. Firefox removes temporary installations when it restarts.

A little less interruption.

Your sentence, uninterrupted.

Write “Bonjour : voici les détails” with ordinary or non-breaking French spaces. Your text stays as you wrote it.

No suggestion-panel flash.

The emoji suggestions Sans Emoji recognizes stay hidden while you type, even when the editor takes a moment to dismiss them.

Emoji when you choose.

Existing emoji and the toolbar emoji picker stay available. Colon searches such as :smile are suppressed too; use the picker when you want to insert an emoji.

Made for Atlassian sites.

Runs on HTTPS atlassian.net, atlassian.com and bitbucket.org pages and their subdomains. Custom company domains and other websites are outside its scope.

Related Atlassian requests.

These Atlassian feature requests describe the typing problem and the desire to turn off emoji autocomplete:

  • JRACLOUD-39164 — Disable colon-triggered emoji suggestions in Jira Cloud.
  • CONFSERVER-92667 — Disable emoji autocomplete independently of other shortcuts in Confluence.

The Confluence request is marked fixed in version 9.1.0, as checked on 13 October 2026. It is included as background; Sans Emoji remains limited to the supported domains above and does not cover custom or self-hosted domains.

No settings to configure.

Once installed, Sans Emoji works automatically on supported pages. There is no toolbar popup to open.

To turn it off, disable it in your browser’s extension manager and refresh the page. Atlassian editors can change; if suggestions still appear or a key behaves unexpectedly, start with the troubleshooting guide.

Your words.
In your browser.

Sans Emoji checks editor content locally to recognize autocomplete. It makes no network requests, stores no data and has no analytics or telemetry.

Your Atlassian site continues to handle your writing as usual. Read what the extension accesses and why
