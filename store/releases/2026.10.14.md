## Public version notes

### English

Fixed a brief page jump when colon-triggered emoji suggestions are created or removed. Hidden suggestions no longer add temporary scrollbars, including when Jira finishes rendering a popup after its query field has disappeared. Your typed colon remains visible, and the manual emoji picker remains available.

### Français

Correction d’un bref décalage de la page à l’apparition ou à la fermeture des suggestions d’emoji déclenchées par les deux-points. Les suggestions masquées n’ajoutent plus de barres de défilement temporaires, y compris lorsque Jira termine l’affichage du panneau après la disparition du champ de recherche. Les deux-points saisis restent visibles et le sélecteur manuel d’emoji reste disponible.

## Submission steps

These historical steps were prepared for 2026.10.14. Follow the [2026.10.15 handoff](2026.10.15.md) for the replacement release.

1. Complete the remaining signed-in editor checks listed in the release notes: punctuation spacing variants, Enter/Tab, undo/redo, mentions, slash commands, and the toolbar picker. The recorded Jira check verifies the popup layout fix; browser fixtures cover the wider behavior but do not replace live compatibility checks.
2. In each store's Sans Emoji listing, upload `dist/sans-emoji.zip` as version `2026.10.14`. Read the resulting validation report before submission.
3. In Mozilla Add-ons, upload `dist/sans-emoji-sources.zip` separately and enter the public version notes above. Complete the [private reviewer-note template](../submission.md#private-reviewer-note-template) with working dedicated test credentials, a supported HTTPS sample editor, and exact login steps. Keep those credentials out of public notes and both archives.
4. Review listing copy, versioned images, privacy answers, and public links. The prepared screenshots are explicitly labeled local fixtures. They do not show a private Jira project or establish live product compatibility.
5. Submit only after the remaining review items are complete. Record the submission status and eventual approval separately; building these ZIPs does not publish them.

The homepage, privacy policy, and support URLs in [the submission reference](../submission.md#public-links-and-contact) returned HTTP 200 over HTTPS without authentication on October 14, 2026. Local website documentation changes have not been deployed by this preparation.
