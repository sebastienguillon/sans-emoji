## Public version notes

### English

Fixed brief page jumps and temporary scrollbars when hidden colon-triggered emoji suggestions are created or removed, including suggestions that outlive their query field. Reduced work during typing and editor updates, especially on large pages and in Firefox. Fixed cancellation when a query field becomes editable after appearing, and fixed Firefox updates to suggestion panels around formatted text. Your typed colon remains visible, and the manual emoji picker remains available.

Known limitations: an editor that rejects synthetic Escape may still intercept Enter or Tab; simultaneous autocomplete menus are not independently scoped while a colon query exists. An error panel without emoji items can appear after its query disappears. Atlassian markup changes can affect support, and custom company domains remain outside scope.

### Français

Correction des brefs décalages de page et des barres de défilement temporaires lors de l’apparition ou de la fermeture des suggestions d’emoji masquées, y compris lorsque celles-ci restent présentes après la disparition du champ de recherche. Réduction du travail effectué pendant la saisie et les mises à jour de l’éditeur, surtout sur les grandes pages et dans Firefox. Correction de l’annulation lorsqu’un champ de recherche devient modifiable après son apparition, ainsi que de la mise à jour des panneaux de suggestions autour du texte mis en forme dans Firefox. Les deux-points saisis restent visibles et le sélecteur manuel d’emoji reste disponible.

Limites connues : un éditeur qui refuse les événements Échap synthétiques peut encore intercepter Entrée ou Tabulation ; les menus de saisie simultanés ne sont pas gérés indépendamment tant qu’une recherche d’emoji est active. Un panneau d’erreur sans emoji peut apparaître après la disparition de son champ de recherche. Les changements du balisage Atlassian peuvent affecter la compatibilité et les domaines personnalisés des entreprises restent hors périmètre.

## Validation and remaining checks

Use the [versioned release notes](../../release-notes/2026.10.15.md#validation) for the checks actually completed against this release. Earlier checks recorded for 2026.10.14 or an intermediate review build do not establish validation of the final 2026.10.15 build.

- Complete the signed-in editor matrix with this build: `Bonjour : suite`, both non-breaking-space variants, Enter/Tab, undo/redo, mentions, slash commands, and the toolbar picker. Record the browser and editor tested. The earlier Jira check covered the popup layout fix; it does not establish live compatibility for the new controller. Jira, Confluence, and Bitbucket checks remain pending for this release unless the versioned notes record otherwise.
- Browser fixtures use the actual unpacked extension in Chromium and inject the compiled script and stylesheet in Firefox. They do not verify Firefox add-on installation or signing. A real Firefox add-on run of the final build and checks on the Chrome 120 and Firefox 140 minimum versions remain pending unless recorded in the versioned notes. Desktop Firefox 140+ is required; Android distribution is not enabled.
- Performance measurements were made on a synthetic page in headless Chromium and Firefox. No live tenant was benchmarked. Chromium's document-wide style recalculation after relevant DOM changes remains a known cost.
- The modern hiding rule applies while any `[data-query-prefix=":"]` element exists; Atlaskit uses that attribute on its editable query field. This also affects simultaneous modern menus elsewhere in the document. Error panels without emoji items may appear after their query disappears and before the editor removes them.

## Submission steps

1. Complete the remaining editor and browser checks above and record their results in the versioned release notes. If packaged source files change, run `pnpm package` again and regenerate the archive checksums.
2. In each store's Sans Emoji listing, upload `dist/sans-emoji.zip` as version `2026.10.15`. Read the resulting validation report before submission.
3. In Mozilla Add-ons, upload `dist/sans-emoji-sources.zip` separately and enter the public version notes above. Complete the [private reviewer-note template](../submission.md#private-reviewer-note-template) with working dedicated test credentials, a supported HTTPS sample editor, and exact login steps. Keep those credentials out of public notes and both archives.
4. Review listing copy, versioned images, privacy answers, and the public homepage, privacy policy, and support URLs in [the submission reference](../submission.md#public-links-and-contact). The screenshots are labeled local fixtures and do not establish live product compatibility. Preserve the abandoned release's images and [capture manifest](../assets/2026.10.14-capture.json) as historical records.
5. Submit after the remaining review items are complete. Record submission, signing, and approval status separately. No store submission or website deployment is recorded by this handoff.
